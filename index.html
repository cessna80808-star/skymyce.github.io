    FAQ

    Good questions.

    What do I need to run Sky-Myce?

    Fabric for Minecraft 26.1.2, Java 25, and the standalone mod JAR from the latest release. Local features need nothing else.

    Does it work without Discord?

    Yes. Dungeon tools, tracking, HUD widgets, and quality-of-life features are local and offline-friendly. Discord only unlocks optional community features like Global Chat, shared rankings, and cosmetics sharing — and you can opt out per category.

    Will it hurt my frame rate?

    Sky-Myce measures its own impact. Graphics Auto lowers its menu effects while your frame rate is under target, then restores them when frames recover. HUD information, dungeon tracking, routing, and the Crush Camera are never reduced.

    How do the leaderboards work?

    Open /sm leaderboard in game. Boards cover economy, dungeons, and RNG across all-time, weekly, and monthly periods. Only opt-in players appear, sharing categories can be disabled independently, and anonymous names stay anonymous.

    Is Sky-Myce affiliated with Hypixel?

    No. Sky-Myce is an independent client-side mod and is not affiliated with Hypixel or Mojang.
